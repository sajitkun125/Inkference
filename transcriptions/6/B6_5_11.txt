a fire on. They had Spears & Beckets as in Tanna &
clubs with stars; & likewise a weapon in the shape of
a Scythe but with short handle, or like a Pick-ax. Their slings
are singular, with oblong heavy Stones, pointed on both
sides, made I believe on purpose. The Genitals of these
people are wrapped in a piece of cloth & some tie the
Cape to the String round the waist, others let it hang down
some have so long a piece of cloth of bark, that it
goes under the Chin & is there tucked under a
hoop, which many wear round the Neck. After we came
[margin: Th. 76° / 4 o'clock P.M.]
to an Anchor, we went to dinner & a great many
of these people came on board, & saw us dine, &
were very desirous of eating Yams, but Pork or wine
they would not taste. They admired every thing we had
but gave scarce any thing in return, for red cloth,
or beads. After dinner we went ashore, with two boats
& about 12 marines. When we landed we found a great
number of people ashore, some armed, some not.
some wore Caps of black Stuff with a red piece of bird-
feathers & some other feathers of look on top. I observed
a curious Spear, whereon a curious face was carved in
the grotesque manner. We asked for water & they pointed
round the East-point a good way. We therefore
took leave of them & went in the boats accompa-
nied by two Natives towards the watering place.
We went over a deal of Shoal ground along a
shore lined with Mangroves, of more than one kind
We saw there several Inlets & went up an Arm
& observed a good many. Ducks of which two were
