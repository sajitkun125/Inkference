ashore towards the Chiefs houses & the latter towards
the Sea. Capt. Cook let go the first hand & was nearly
drawn into the Sea by the latter, when Capt. Cook said
he would not go into the Sea, Towha let his hand go
& went away, & we returned into the boat & counted
going along the range of Canoes, there numerous
boats which amounted to 159 double Canoes, & about
70 smaller ones with thatched houses, for to receive the
chiefs during night, & to carry provisions for the Fleet
so that the whole amounted to 229. When we came on
board we inquired where this armament was inten-
ded for, but the people gave us vague accounts: for
they said only in 10 months the Expedition would take
place against Tareetabooooe the Chief of Moria;
which is a Whennooa in Imaia. But Potatou
said to M.r Pickersgill & me, that Eames, Chief
of Tapira, was fallen off & that he was gone
over to Eeatooa & that this Armament
was intended against him, under Towha's
command & that the Expedition was against
Etu's will. But
