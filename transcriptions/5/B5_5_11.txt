of revue or Exercise; we saw Otu at Opara, but not Towha,
& we returned soon on board. We got so many hogs on board
that we could not well keep them, for the fat ones would
not eat & became sick & we were obliged to kill
them as fast as possible. However the next morning we
returned to Opara & saw Otu again & found likewise
Towha there; & after all we brought them both of on board
the Ship for dinner, where they both visited every part
of the Ship above & below decks. Towha, who never had
seen a Ship before, was quite stupefied with admiration.
April y.e 28.th   When they were coming on board, these two Men
both told us that the Men of Morea & those of Tiarabu
were their Ennemies, & desired Capt. Cook should
with his Ship & fire the Guns against them both;
which proves the former accounts both to be true.
In the Afternoon I stood on board & revised some
descriptions. If the weather proves fair I intend
one of these days, to undertake an Expedition
up the hills & lay a night out, & try to rummage
the woods & sides of the higher hills for plants
& birds, as we find so little (new) below. The next morning
I took a walk on shore with my son, my Servant & the
Cook of our Mess who all are sick & can bear but little
exercise, we rambled gently on over the plains of Matavai.
April y.e 29.th   In the Afternoon I went with M.r Sparrmann
& two Men to attend me, up the hills in order to
reach their Summits, which had been attempted by the
people in the Endeavour but they never could do it. I had
Noona a friendly faithfull Indian boy with me, & was
followed by a crowd of Natives, who when they saw that
I was really going far up the hills soon returned.
